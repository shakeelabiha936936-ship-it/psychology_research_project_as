 Factors Affecting Human Behavior
Introduction
In this project, I want to explain some important factors that affect human behavior. Psychology helps us understand why people think, feel, and act differently. I chose this topic because human behavior is something we see in our daily life.
1. Emotions:
Emotions play an important role in our behavior. When we are happy, we usually feel more active and positive. When we are sad or angry, our behavior may change.
2. Motivation:
Motivation helps us achieve our goals. For example, when a student wants to get good marks, they work hard and focus on their studies.
3. Environment:
Our environment also affects our behavior. Family, friends, school, and society influence how we think and act.
4. Stress:
Stress can affect our attention and learning. For example, when students have too many assignments, they may feel stressed and find it difficult to concentrate.
5. Learning and Experience:
We learn many things from our experiences. Our past experiences can change how we behave in different situations.

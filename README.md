 Introduction:
Psychology is the scientific study of human behavior and mental processes. This research project helps us understand how people think, feel, learn, and behave in different situations.

Research Objectives:
To understand human thoughts, feelings, and behavior.
To study how stress affects students' academic performance.
To explore the role of motivation in learning.
To understand how emotions affect decision-making.
To study the importance of attention and memory in daily life.

Research Methods
In this project, we use simple research methods such as:
Observation: Watching and understanding people's behavior.
Survey: Asking people questions about their experiences.
Questionnaire: Collecting information through written questions.
Interview: Talking to people to understand their thoughts and feelings.

Expected Outcomes:
This research is expected to help us understand human behavior, emotions, learning, and motivation. It may also help us identify factors that affect students' academic performance.
